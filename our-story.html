OUR STORY

Inspired by a Legend.

Honouring a Legacy.

Maestro Paints is more than just a brand, it is a legacy. Created in loving memory of Mohamed Adeel Aboobaker, Maestro was inspired by a man whose vision, passion and determination transformed a humble hardware store in the Durban CBD into one of the industry's respected names.

Maestro Paints is the fulfilment of that manufacturing vision and a tribute to the passion, integrity and excellence he stood for.

Premium paints and coatings for every surface, built to last.
